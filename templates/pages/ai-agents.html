From pilot to production

How a proof of concept becomes a business capability.

  1. 1

    Select the workflow

    A specific process with clear inputs, a definition of done and a measurable baseline.

  2. 2

    Set the boundaries

    What the agent may do alone, what needs approval and what it must never do, enforced in the system.

  3. 3

    Connect securely

    Access to data and systems through governed interfaces with their own identity and scoped permissions.

  4. 4

    Evaluate

    A test set from real, anonymized cases, run before every change, plus human review in production.

  5. 5

    Operate and improve

    Named owners, cost and quality monitoring, and a regular cycle of measured improvements.

Responsible AI

Governance designed in, not added later.

Enterprise AI has to answer to security teams, auditors and customers. We design agents so those answers are easy to give.

  • {{icon:shield}}Least-privilege access. Agents receive only the data and actions their task requires.
  • {{icon:people}}Human oversight. Material actions are reviewed or approved by accountable people.
  • {{icon:doc}}Traceability. Inputs, decisions, tool calls and approvals are logged for review.
  • {{icon:gauge}}Cost and quality controls. Usage budgets, quality metrics and alerts from day one.
  • {{icon:check}}Data protection. Handling designed around applicable privacy laws in each market.
