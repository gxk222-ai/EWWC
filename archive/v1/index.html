Industry playbooks

Same pattern in every sector: data locked in legacy systems, heavy regulation, manual hand-offs. The workflows and ROI levers differ.

Financial Services

Pain points: core banking and policy systems on mainframes; KYC/AML reviews and loan or claims processing run on swivel-chair work; model-risk and audit scrutiny blocks AI rollout.

Solution: ProbeStack wraps core and payment systems as governed APIs and MCP tools. NexGen runs KYC refresh, credit memo, dispute and claims agents with human approval at defined thresholds and a full decision audit trail.

ROI metrics: cost per KYC review or claim · loan and claims cycle time · straight-through-processing rate · false-positive alert volume · audit findings and time to evidence · integration spend avoided

Healthcare

Pain points: fragmented EHR, payer and scheduling systems; prior authorization and revenue-cycle backlogs; clinician time lost to documentation; HIPAA exposure from ungoverned data access.

Solution: ProbeStack provides governed, least-privilege access to EHR (including FHIR/HL7 interfaces), payer and claims systems. NexGen orchestrates prior-auth, coding, denial-management and referral agents with clinician sign-off and PHI-aware policies.

ROI metrics: prior-authorization turnaround · claim denial rate and days in A/R · administrative hours per clinician · referral leakage · patient wait time · compliance incidents

Hospitality

Pain points: PMS, CRS, POS and loyalty platforms that don't talk to each other; guest requests handled by phone and email; thin margins and seasonal staffing gaps.

Solution: ProbeStack unifies property, booking, loyalty and channel-manager APIs. NexGen runs guest-service, rebooking, upsell and housekeeping-dispatch agents across brands and properties, with spend capped per property.

ROI metrics: RevPAR and ancillary revenue per stay · guest request resolution time · direct-booking share vs OTA commission · staff hours per occupied room · guest satisfaction

GovTech / Public Sector

Pain points: decades-old case management systems; benefits, permits and licensing backlogs; strict procurement, accessibility, transparency and data-sovereignty rules; scarce engineering capacity.

Solution: ProbeStack modernizes legacy systems in place through managed APIs and automated migrations, avoiding big-bang replacements. NexGen runs intake, eligibility pre-screening and case-routing agents with explainable decisions and caseworker approval.

ROI metrics: application backlog and processing time · cost per case · first-contact resolution · modernization spend avoided · audit and FOIA response time · citizen satisfaction

Retail

Pain points: siloed commerce, ERP, inventory and CRM platforms; stockouts and overstock; manual merchandising and pricing; rising service volumes.

Solution: ProbeStack connects commerce, OMS, ERP, supplier and marketplace APIs. NexGen runs replenishment, pricing-recommendation, order-exception and customer-service agents with margin guardrails and human review for price changes.

ROI metrics: stockout and overstock rates · inventory turns · GMROI · order-exception resolution time · cost per service contact · conversion and average order value

Energy

Pain points: OT/IT divide between SCADA, asset and ERP systems; reactive maintenance; manual crew dispatch and outage communication; regulatory reporting load.

Solution: ProbeStack exposes asset, work-order, metering and GIS data through governed interfaces, keeping OT isolated behind read-only or approved-write access. NexGen runs maintenance-triage, dispatch, outage-communication and reporting agents with safety-critical steps held for human approval.

ROI metrics: unplanned downtime · maintenance cost per asset · crew utilization and truck rolls avoided · restoration and notification time (SAIDI/CAIDI) · regulatory reporting hours
