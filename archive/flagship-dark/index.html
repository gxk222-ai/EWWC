04 — Governance by design

Control is not a feature.It is the architecture.

I.

Regulatory alignment

Our controls map to the frameworks your board already answers to, including [EU AI Act, DORA, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST AI RMF]. Evidence is produced as the system runs, not assembled before the audit.

II.

Private enclave computing

Models and agents run inside your perimeter or a dedicated, single-tenant environment in the jurisdiction you choose. Your data does not train shared models and does not leave the boundary you define.

III.

Total auditability

Every prompt, decision, approval and system call is recorded with identity, policy version and cost, in an immutable log you own. Any outcome can be reconstructed, step by step, for any reviewer.
