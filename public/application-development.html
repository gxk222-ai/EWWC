Engineering approach

Quality built into every release.

Working software early and often, with the engineering practices that keep it reliable as it grows.

  • User-centered design. Research, prototypes and usability testing with real users.
  • Iterative delivery. Short cycles with regular demonstrations and feedback.
  • Automated testing. Unit, integration and end-to-end tests in every pipeline.
  • Security by default. Secure coding, dependency scanning and access controls.
  • Performance and accessibility. Measured against agreed targets, including WCAG.
  • Deployment and support. Automated releases, monitoring and ongoing maintenance.
