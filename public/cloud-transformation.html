Cloud and DevOps

Repeatable, observable, cost-aware platforms.

We help teams move to the cloud deliberately and run it well, with automation replacing manual steps wherever it reduces risk.

  • Cloud architecture and migration. Assessment, target design and phased migration plans.
  • AWS and Google Cloud solutions. Platforms to confirm
  • CI/CD pipelines. Automated build, test and release for every change.
  • Infrastructure as Code. Versioned, reviewable, repeatable environments.
  • Containers and deployment automation. Consistent runtime from laptop to production.
  • Observability and reliability. Metrics, logs, traces and actionable alerts.
  • Security and cost optimization. Guardrails, least privilege and regular cost reviews.

Digital transformation and technology consulting

From strategy to sustained change.

Transformation succeeds when priorities are clear, delivery is disciplined and people are brought along.

  • Technology strategy and roadmaps

    Priorities, sequencing and business cases that leadership can commit to.

  • Program and project management

    Plans, governance, risk management and transparent reporting.

  • Agile transformation

    Delivery practices adapted to your organization, not copied from a textbook.

  • Business process optimization

    Simplified processes ready for automation and better tools.

  • Data migration and integration planning

    Data quality, ownership, rehearsal and reconciliation planned up front.

  • Change management and alignment

    Stakeholder engagement, communication and training that drive adoption.

What you receive

  • Readiness assessment
