Who we are

This website is operated by E Worldwide Consulting, headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, United States (legal entity details to be confirmed). In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to E Worldwide Consulting.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, business email, company name, country or region, service of interest, project description and, if you choose, a budget range and timeline. We also record the date and time of your submission.

Our servers keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to operate and secure the website. We use your IP address for a short time to prevent spam and abuse of the contact form.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss potential services.
  • To keep a record of our communications.
  • To protect the website and our business from fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use contact form information for automated decision-making.

Legal basis and consent

We process contact form information with your consent and to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate the website and respond to enquiries (for example, hosting and email providers), under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or where required by law.

International transfers

We work with clients in the United States and South Africa. Your information may be processed in either country. Where information is transferred across borders, we take steps to ensure it remains protected as required by applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Retention

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting relationship, and no longer than [retention period to be confirmed] if no engagement follows.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a regulator, such as the Information Regulator in South Africa. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to submissions.
