How we collaborate

One team across the United States and South Africa.

Every engagement is led by a single accountable lead who works directly with the client. Around that lead, we assemble the disciplines the project needs, whether that is AI engineering, application development, cloud, data or program management.

Teams share one plan, one definition of done and one set of tools, so collaboration works the same way whether people are in Atlanta, South Africa or elsewhere.
