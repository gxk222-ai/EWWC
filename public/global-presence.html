Global delivery

One team across locations.

A flexible approach that brings together business stakeholders, engineers and specialists across the United States and South Africa.

  • A shared working window

    Atlanta and South Africa are six to seven hours apart. The US morning is reserved for joint decisions and reviews.

  • Written context

    Decision logs, acceptance criteria and architecture notes keep work moving between time zones.

  • One definition of done

    The same quality, security and release standards, automated in the pipeline for every location.

  • Local compliance

    Data protection and regulatory needs in each market, including South Africa's POPIA, planned from the start.
