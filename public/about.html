The reach of a global firm. The care of a founder-led one.

E Worldwide Consulting is a technology consulting company headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, helping organizations in the United States and South Africa turn strategy into working technology.

Who we are

Strategy-led. AI-powered. Enterprise-ready. Globally connected.

Founded by Vanessa Ross in 2020, E Worldwide Consulting brings together technology strategy, application engineering, AI and program delivery. We work with business and technology leaders who need more than advice: they need solutions that reach production and keep delivering value.

We combine the structured approach of an enterprise advisory firm with the agility and personal accountability of a founder-led company. Clients work directly with senior people who care about the outcome.

Our focus today is on four connected practices: AI agents and intelligent automation, enterprise application development, cloud and DevOps, and digital transformation consulting.

How we work

What clients can expect.

  • Business first

    We understand your goals, users and constraints before recommending technology.

  • Accountability

    Clear ownership, transparent reporting and honest conversations about risk.

  • Outcomes over activity

    Success measured by business results agreed at the start.

  • Responsible innovation

    AI and automation applied where they help, with governance built in.

  • Partnership

    We work alongside your teams and leave them stronger.

  • Global perspective

    Delivery that draws on people and experience across markets.
